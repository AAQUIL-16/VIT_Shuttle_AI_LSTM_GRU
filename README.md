The Intelligent Passenger-Centric Shuttle Recommendation System is designed to improve shuttle travel decisions by addressing the limitation of conventional shuttle systems that mainly provide location and estimated arrival time without considering whether a passenger can actually board the shuttle. The proposed system uses GPS/GNSS-based mobility data collected from the shuttle through an ESP32 and transmitted using 4G LTE to a cloud platform. An LSTM/GRU-based model predicts shuttle arrival time, while a latent occupancy inference mechanism analyzes mobility characteristics such as dwell duration and inter-stop travel behaviour to estimate the shuttle's hidden crowding state and probability of successful boarding without dedicated passenger-counting hardware. An Explainable Prescriptive Arbitration (EPA) mechanism then combines the predicted ETA, boarding probability, waiting and travel costs, alternative shuttle information, and walking cost to recommend one of three actions: Board, Wait, or Walk. The system continuously updates its recommendation as new shuttle observations are received and presents the information through a Flutter-based mobile application. The key novelty is the integration of hardware-free latent occupancy inference, boarding probability estimation, ETA prediction, and explainable passenger-level decision-making into a unified IoT-AI shuttle recommendation system.
